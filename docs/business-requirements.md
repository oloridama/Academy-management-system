**Admission**
A staff is contacted by a prospect who wants to become a student. After they are briefed on the requirements, they decide if they want to proceed with the next steps of registration. Whoever attends to the prospect is the admission officer. So therefore they have to input their name in the admission officer part of the form. For admission process to be completed, the prospect must provide on the requirements details to the application form.
